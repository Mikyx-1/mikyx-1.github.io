Le Hoang Viet's Homepage

I'm an AI engineer at MonoRevo Inc., a Japanese startup based in Ho Chi Minh City, where I work on vision–language models, OCR, similarity retrieval, and optimisation.

Before this, I was a computer vision engineer at Ftech, working on image segmentation and optimisation, and before that an AI engineer at Bosch Global Software Technologies, working on object detection, activity recognition, and edge computing.

I received my B.Eng. in Automation & Control from the Ho Chi Minh University of Technology, VNU-HCM. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning algorithms and high-performance systems — models that are not only accurate, but computationally efficient and production-ready.

My interests are computer vision (vision–language models and compact architectures for low-resource environments), efficient computing (finding bottlenecks in deep learning systems and fixing them through fusion, sampling, quantisation, and heterogeneous computing), and reinforcement learning (training efficiency and convergence in complex environments).

I'm happy to hear about collaborations and hard problems at the intersection of AI and high-performance systems.
